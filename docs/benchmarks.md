## Method

- **Corpus:** Xiph.org derf CIF sequences (akiyo = low motion, foreman = medium, mobile = high), first 100 frames, 4:2:0; audio is 30 s mono 48 kHz excerpts of Sintel's trailer mix and Elephants Dream's music.
- **Video metric:** every codec's output is decoded and scored by the *same* ffmpeg PSNR and SSIM filters against the source. Rate is the raw payload, with no container overhead on either side. Each codec is swept over its quality setting; a single point means little, the curve is the result.
- **Reference ceiling:** no true rate-distortion bound exists for natural video, so the practical ceiling is the best of x264 `veryslow`, x265 `veryslow` and SVT-AV1 preset 4.
- **Equal-quality column:** how many times *larger* the tpt stream is than the best reference at the same luma PSNR (log-linear interpolation). `>1` means tpt is worse; `n/a` means the PSNR is outside the reference's range.
- **Audio:** Aura is lossless, so the comparison is size against FLAC and against an *estimate* of an ideal predictor (order-32 LPC per 4096-sample block, ideal Laplace entropy coder, side info ignored). It is an estimate, not a proven bound.
- **Cinder (lossy audio) is not benchmarked yet.** It has a known ≈3 dB SNR ceiling until the v2 overlap-add redesign lands (see `docs/codec-design.md`); once it does, the plan is rate-quality vs Opus plus the Gaussian reverse-water-filling bound on the MDCT coefficients.
